# FODiff

Official repository for:

**Towards High-Perception Demoiréing: A Perception-Oriented 4K Benchmark and Frequency-decoupled One-step Diffusion Model**

Accepted by **IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology (TCSVT), 2026**.

## Overview

We propose **FODiff**, a perception-oriented one-step diffusion framework for high-resolution image demoiréing.

The main idea is to decouple frequency information during restoration, suppress ambiguous high-frequency moiré components, and preserve useful structural and texture information for diffusion-based reconstruction.

We also introduce **PODM**, a perception-oriented 4K demoiréing benchmark for training and evaluating real-world demoiréing methods.
